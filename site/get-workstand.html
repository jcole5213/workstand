Workstand

Know when your chain needs wax before it squeaks.

Workstand is a maintenance log for your bikes. It reads your rides from Strava and counts ride hours on every part, so it can tell you what's due: rewax, quick link, tires, sealant, pads, fork and shock. It runs in your own Claude account and only you can see it.

What it tracks

Wear by ride hours, not guesswork

Parts count Strava moving time, so a slow muddy hour counts the same as a fast road hour. Every interval below is a starting point you can change per bike.

Chain waxevery 10 hWarns you when the next ride is longer than what's left.
Quick link5 usesEach rewax counts a use, so you have a spare ready.
Chain wear0.5% · 0.75%Log a checker reading and it tells you when to replace.
Tiresfront / rear hoursSuggests a front-to-rear rotation on road and gravel.
Sealant90 daysRuns on the calendar, shorter in summer heat.
Pads, fork, shock40–85 h · 50 hPad checks by terrain, lower-leg and air-can service.

Set up in about five minutes

A Claude account

Workstand is a Claude artifact. Your copy, your data and your bike art live in your account.

A Strava subscription

Strava's Claude connector only works with a paid Strava subscription. Without one, Workstand can't read your rides.

  1. Connect Strava to Claude

    1. On claude.ai, open Settings, then Connectors.
    2. Choose Browse connectors, search for Strava, and select Connect.
    3. Sign in to Strava and select Authorize. Workstand only reads your gear and activities.

    Menu names can shift a little between Claude versions. If you don't see Strava, search the connector list.

  2. Paste the setup prompt

    Copy the prompt, paste it into a new chat in Claude, and send it. Claude publishes your own private copy and gives you a link.

    Please set up Workstand for me. It's a bike maintenance log that runs as a Claude artifact.

1. Fetch https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jcole5213/workstand/HEAD/workstand.html and read the whole file.
2. Publish it unchanged as a new artifact in my account, with the icon "bike" and these capabilities:
   {"db": {}, "user": {}, "assets": {}, "mcp": {"servers": [{"server": "Strava", "tools": ["get_gear", "search_activities"]}]}}
3. Give me the link. Don't add any data. The page walks me through connecting Strava, picking my bikes and adding bike art.

  3. Open your link and follow the setup

    1. Allow Strava when the page asks. It loads the bikes in your Strava gear.
    2. Pick your bikes and say whether each is road, gravel or mountain.
    3. Choose a start date. Counters begin there, so pick the day you last waxed or fitted new parts.
    4. Add bike art. Use the built-in pixel bike, upload your own pixel art, or let it pixelize a photo. Drag the part markers onto the chain, tires and brakes.

    Bookmark the link. That page is your Workstand from then on.

Questions
Can anyone else see my garage?

No. Your copy is private to your Claude account, and the data is stored under your own account inside it. Nobody who shared this page with you can see your rides or logs.

Does it change anything on Strava?

No. It only reads your bikes and your activities. It never posts, edits or deletes anything.

How do I get a newer version later?

Paste this into Claude with your own link: "Update my Workstand artifact at <your link> to the latest version. Read it first, then fetch https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jcole5213/workstand/HEAD/workstand.html and republish it to the same link, unchanged, keeping the same capabilities." Your data stays where it is.

The page says Strava isn't available.

Check that the Strava connector shows as connected in Claude's settings and that your Strava subscription is active. Then reload your Workstand and select Connect Strava again.
